# Luxembourgish adjectives for petname generation.
#
# One adjective per line as three colon-separated, gender-inflected forms in the
# articleless strong nominative:
#
#     masculine:feminine:neuter
#
# e.g. `groussen:grouss:grousst`. The feminine is the bare lemma; the masculine
# adds -en (with sandhi: rout->rouden, déif->déiwen, héich->héijen) and the
# neuter adds -t, except after a stem-final -t or -d, where nothing is added
# (gutt:gutt, breet:breet, midd:midd). The generator drops a final -n/-nn at a
# join per the Eifeler Regel, so only these citation forms are stored.
#
# A unit test (`small_adjectives_are_regularly_formed`) checks that the masculine
# ends in -n and that the neuter follows the rule above, so a slip here fails the
# build rather than showing up in generated names. Real exceptions can be listed
# in that test.
#
# Lines beginning with `#`, and anything after a `#` on a line, are ignored.
#
# ⚠️  SEED LIST — best-effort, authored by a non-native speaker. Native-speaker
# review of forms and genders is needed; correct and extend freely.
